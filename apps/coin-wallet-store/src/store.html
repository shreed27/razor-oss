The buyer journey

From button to verified payment

Apna hosts checkout and matches incoming bank notifications to the active payment session.

01

Create a session

The merchant backend creates an order with a fixed amount and a unique checkout session.

02

Pay with UPI

The buyer scans the session QR or opens a supported UPI app to pay the receiving account.

03

Verify the result

When configured, bank SMS or Gmail alerts are matched to the order before it is marked successful.

Inside the demo

Payment tools, in one place

Some capabilities need extra setup—such as a connected bank observer, WhatsApp provider, or merchant configuration.

▦

Dynamic UPI checkout

Checkout sessions carry the order amount and show a session-specific payment QR.

📩

Bank notification matching

SMS and Gmail observers can send bank alerts to the payment verification service.

🛡️

Payment risk checks

Order and device signals can be used by configured fraud and review flows.

🧾

Payment outcomes

See success, failure, review, expiry, and cancellation pages further down.

Before trying a real payment: confirm the receiving UPI account is yours or that you have permission to use it. The demo amount is not a sandbox charge; it can move real money. Automatic success confirmation needs the corresponding bank observer to be online and configured.

Create a payment link from chat.

Connect the Apna MCP server to an MCP-compatible assistant, then ask it to create a payment link and QR. This is for a configured Apna instance; the merchant and receiving UPI account need to exist first.
